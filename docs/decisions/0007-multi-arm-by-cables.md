# 0007. Multi-arm by cables; ● arms one, holding adds

**Date:** 2026-10-15 (the rooms specs, 821f184e; the flow spec, decision
11).

## Decision

Recording several inputs to several tracks at once is set up by the rig's
cables. In the Studio, ● arms one track, and holding ● adds one. Touch does
what the keys do.

## Why

● had toggled, which made arming one track at a time the only path and
multi-arm a mode of its own.

## What it rules out

A multi-arm mode separate from the cables; ● toggling.
