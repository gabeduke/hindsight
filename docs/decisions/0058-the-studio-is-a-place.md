# 0058. The studio is a place

**Date:** 2026-10-24 (`the-studio-space.md`, agreed with Gabe).

## Decision

The drawn room becomes where you are, not what you look at. The Studio's
desk and the Listening Room's shelf wall are two walls of one space;
switching rooms turns a camera between them while the header and the bar
hold still. The Listening Room is a library: the shelf wall and the List.
The drawn room is a backdrop, not a surface: nothing on it is pressed.

## Gabe's words

*"a lot of wasted space that could be dedicated to organization of the music
library … A more 3 dimensional feel would help with my idea to make this
more of a studio feel"*; *"Visuals and animations [that] feel fluid and
immersive rather than just another mobile app."*

## Why

The Listening Room had a lot of wasted space that could organize the
library, and the app should feel like a studio, not another mobile app.

## What it rules out

Controls on the drawn room; a 3D engine for it ([decision 0010](0010-flat-first.md)).
