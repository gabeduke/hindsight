## What landed in 2026-10-16

1. **The F1–F9 flow spec and rooms branches merged to `main`:**
   - Two nouns (`takes` and `tapes`), one way to listen (`tap plays`, `Open`
     at `wave.html` to dig in), `BOUNCE` replacing Mix down and Cut a record,
     `clip` → `part` in rooms wording, and the crate/records/chair/TOOLS removed.
   - Room scene furniture (`lib/room/stage.js`, `lib/room/space.js`), rope cables
     on the Rig (`lib/rig/route.js`, `lib/scene/cable.js`), and shelf-to-deck
     cassette flight (`lib/room/fly.js`, `lib/scene/flight.js`).

2. **Architecture & UX flow overhaul (`specs/2026-10-16-rooms-architecture-and-flow-design.md`):**
   - **60fps tape & deck visualizations:** `lib/tape/page.js` now runs
     `playheadLoop` and `heardNow()` on `lib/objects/motion.js`'s shared `ticker`
     (`Loop`), interpolating the lane playhead, ruler cursor, overview cursor,
     and LCD counter at 60fps between 200ms polls (including loop wrap-around).
     `lib/room/doorway.js` mounts a physical `ReelWindow` on the console deck
     driven by `NowPlaying.onChange` and `NowPlaying.snapshot` instead of static
     CSS keyframes or a 500ms DOM-scraping `setInterval`.
   - **Zero-FOUC frame-0 flag gating:** `:root[data-rooms]` in `web/static/styles.css`
     hides legacy containers (`.shelf-tools`, `#takes`, `#takes-empty`,
     `#take-detail`, `.about`) on frame 0 before any async module import runs.
   - **Top-right header utility cluster (`.appbar-end`):** When `hindsight.rooms`
     is on, `lib/nav.js` (`mountHeaderTools`) places consistent `.appbar-btn`
     pills (`Releases / Version`, `Guide`, `Settings`) in the top-right header on
     every page, backed by `ensureSettingsSheet` (`lib/settings.js`) and
     `ensureUpdateSheet` (`lib/update.js`).
   - **Unified Media Player (`#np`) with 3-way source switcher (`Feed` · `Tape` · `Shelf`):**
     `lib/bar/feed-source.js`, `lib/bar/take-keys.js`, `lib/bar/bar.js`, and
     `lib/rooms/studio.js` expose a 3-way segmented source selector on `#np` in
     both the Listening Room and the Studio. `Feed` monitors the live studio ring
     (`/api/status` + `/api/envelope`), lets you scrub back into the ring buffer,
     and provides a one-tap `GRAB` button (`#np-feed-grab`) into the Studio's
     buffer. Navigating from the Listening Room into the Studio with a playing
     Shelf take seamlessly continues playback via `pageCarry()`.
   - **Inline Sketchbook in the Listening Room (`#room-sketchbook`):** Clicking
     `✎` (`#np-note`) or the Sketchbook on the side table in `takes.html` opens
     an inline `#room-sketchbook` sheet (`lib/room/doorway.js`) without leaving
     the page or interrupting `<audio>` playback. Supports adding/editing/removing
     timestamped notes, seeking (`✎ 0:42`), grabbing a note's span into the
     Studio buffer (`GRAB`), and renaming, starring (`★`), or tagging the take.
   - **Collapsible Studio swimlanes & Instrument inside `#np`:** `#tracks-fold-btn`
     (`Tracks ▾ / ▸`) in `tape.html` and `lib/tape/page.js` folds/unfolds `#lanes`
     (persisted in `sessionStorage` and auto-unfolding on `hindsight:tape-changed`).
     `lib/play/strip.js` mounts `#play-strip` inside `<section id="np">` as the
     Media Player's upper performance deck.
   - **Automated architectural guardrails (`web/static/lib/architecture.test.js`):**
     Enforces no DOM scraping (`readBar`, `readRows`), no synthetic `.click()`
     bridging (`clickControl`), `ReelWindow` usage, 60fps `Loop` interpolation,
     frame-0 `:root[data-rooms]` CSS gating, and `#play-strip` inside `#np`.
