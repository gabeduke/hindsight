# F2 · Bounce — implementation plan

**Goal:** With the rooms on, the Studio's ⋯ offers **Bounce the loop** and
**Bounce the whole tape** in place of *Mix down* ×2, its toast has **▶** and
**Open**, and the tape's menu keeps **Last bounce**. *Cut a record*, the
Records shelf, the turntable, the sleeves and `record.js` go; a take that
carries `Meta.Record` is an ordinary take.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-flow-design.md` — the verbs table,
both words tables, *The Studio: one selection, two verbs* (the BOUNCE bullet),
decisions 2 and 5, *What can be dropped*, and *Build order* F2. Evidence: the
walkthrough's story 7 and ranking #6. Changes the code of P5
(`lib/room/`) and the scene contract (the `record` kind).

**Branch:** `rooms/flow-f2`, off `rooms/flow-f1`; PR base `rooms/flow-f1`
(retarget before merging). Nothing merged to `main`.

## Global Constraints

- Behind `hindsight.rooms`. With it off, the tape page keeps *Mix down the
  loop* / *Mix down the whole tape* and their toasts, word for word.
- No server change and no new route: BOUNCE is the existing
  `POST /api/tapes/mixdown` (`{all}`), the one Mix down sends today. A bounce
  is already a take with `origin: "tape"` and its `tape_id`, which is how its
  tape's family finds it (`lib/shelf.js` `fold`): nothing to attach.
- `Meta.Record` and the Go that reads it stay: they are the server's, nothing
  in the rooms sends them any more, and a take that has them is not
  migrated or hidden.
- Every new test is seen failing first (module absent, or the old code put
  back); the commit says so.
- `sw.js` → `hindsight-shell-v100`; new `lib/` files go in `SHELL`, deleted
  ones come out.
- New CSS is scoped to what it styles.

## Decisions the spec left open

- **▶ in the Studio.** The Studio's bar holds the tape; a take in the bar
  there is F5's (*A take played there has the bar until it ends or ⏏*) and
  F4's hand-off. Until then ▶ plays the take through a one-off `<audio>` on
  this device (its preview, like every list row) with a **■** toast to stop
  it; it never touches the tape. F5 swaps that for the bar.
- **Last bounce is a menu line that raises the same toast** (▶ and Open) for
  the newest bounce of the loaded tape, rather than a menu row holding two
  buttons (a `menuitem` can't hold buttons). It reads `Last bounce` and is
  disabled, saying why, until the tape has one.
- **Which take is a tape's last bounce:** the newest take with
  `origin: "tape"` and this tape's `tape_id`; one made before `tape_id`
  existed counts when its label is the tape's name and no other tape owns
  that label (the same rule `fold` uses).
- **The tape page of a tape in the chair** (`wave.html?tape=`) loses *Cut a
  record* and gets nothing in its place: BOUNCE is the Studio's ⋯ only (the
  chair itself goes in F6).
- **Records on the shelf:** a take with `record` folds into its source's or
  its tape's family like any other, and appears on the This-month / Archive
  shelves by its date.
