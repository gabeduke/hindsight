# F9 · The guide, in step with the rooms — implementation plan

**Goal:** `docs/guide.md` (embedded in the binary, drawn by `guide.html`) says
what the two rooms are, in their words, and no sentence about the rooms is
false any more. With the rooms off, the guide's description of the old app
stays true: the guide has always described both, the old app unmarked and the
rooms behind *With the two rooms on*.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-flow-design.md` — *The guide's
Words, planned*, the F9 row, *The ten stories, after*. **Branch:**
`rooms/f9-guide` off `rooms/all` (5a79ad8). PR base `rooms/all`. No Go, no JS
behaviour; the guide, `words.test.js` and this plan. `CACHE` -> v110 because a
test under `web/static/` changes (AGENTS.md).

## Decisions

- **The convention is made explicit.** The guide's intro says: unmarked text is
  the app with the rooms off; a passage that opens *With the two rooms on*
  (and the sections that are the rooms') is the rooms. That is what the test
  reads.
- **The words section is §8.14**, linked from §2 and from the rooms' preview
  sections; §2 itself keeps the flag-off words (the spec: it becomes the table
  only when the rooms are the default).
- **Open gets a section** (§4.1, under the take page) because the page the
  guide calls the take page is, with the rooms on, a different page.
- **Walkthroughs (§8.15):** six of the spec's ten stories (keep it, lift a
  moment, listen back and jot, reuse a bit, finish it, tidy up), as buttons.
  Each step was run in the demo, rooms on, and its button names read off the
  page; the gesture counts stay in the spec.
