# F5a · A tap plays; holding selects; the sheet goes; the deck is the bar — implementation plan

**Goal:** With `hindsight.rooms` on, a tap on a take in the Listening Room plays
it in the bar and nothing opens; a hold (or ⇧-click, or the Select key)
selects, and a selection can be given a tag; the spine's sheet is gone; the
room's deck is a picture of the bar with no keys of its own; tapes are no longer
on the room's shelf. With the flag off: nothing changes.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-flow-design.md` — *One way to
listen* → *A tap plays*, *It keeps playing as you move around*, *What happens
to today's surfaces*, the words tables (*deck, player, dock*, *PUT IT ON, the
deck's ▶, Play from…*, *SIT DOWN…*, *tape/record*), stories 1, 5, 8, the
Listening Room row, decisions 6 and 8, *Answered by Gabe* 2, *Build order* F5.

**Split.** F5 is two PRs. **F5a (this):** a tap plays; holding selects, with
Tag; the sheet goes; the deck drawn from the bar; tapes per answer 2.
**F5b (later, not here):** the bar's strip on a phone, its name → Open, ✎, and
R2's sound at the tap with its flight. `rooms/r2-shelf-to-deck` (#116) is not
merged here.

**Branch:** `rooms/flow-f5a` off `rooms/all` (c1dec00). PR base `rooms/all`
("retarget before merging; never merge to main"). `CACHE` → `hindsight-shell-v106`.

## Decisions the spec left open

- **One tap function** (`lib/room/tap.js`) for every surface: a spine, a row of
  the List, a month's row, a search result (the same two), a family line (a
  List row). It loads the take in the bar if the bar holds another, then
  toggles the row's player. A take still encoding says so and does nothing.
  The player is fetched as the finger goes down (`warm`), so the toggle is in
  the press on a phone.
- **A take that ended stays in the bar**, paused at its end, ▶ lit: the deck
  keeps it, and ▶ again plays it (the old PUT IT ON did the same). The
  Capture shelf's "back to the tape at the end" is not copied.
- **The hold.** The app's `SELECT_HOLD_MS` (500 ms), **touch and pen only**; a
  mouse's press never selects (it would catch a slow click). Mouse: **⇧-click**,
  or the List's **Select** key. Keyboard: **⇧-Enter / ⇧-Space** on a spine or
  row. Plain Enter/Space is a tap (plays). With a selection on, any tap, click,
  Enter or Space **toggles**; nothing plays. Moving more than 8 px, a scroll
  or a cancel ends a hold and selects nothing; a hold's own lift is swallowed
  so it does not also play.
- **The selection is the rack's** (`takes.selecting`, `takes.selected`, the
  select bar): the room's spines mirror it (a `selected` class, `aria-pressed`)
  through a new `onSelected` callback on `TakesList`. The select bar shows over
  the room's foot exactly as it does over the List.
- **Tag** is a fourth key on that bar, made in script by the room (so the
  flag-off page has no such markup). It opens a small dialog of the tags (a
  chip each, the tag every selected take already wears lit), *No tag*, and
  *+ New tag* / *Edit tags* (the existing manager; a tag made there is
  applied). Pressing a tag writes `{tag}` to every selected take with the
  existing `PATCH /api/take`, then refreshes once. A failure counts, as ★ does.
- **`?take=NAME`** (a toast's *Name it*, a held Capture spine) opened the sheet
  to name it. With the rooms on it goes to Open (`wave.html?file=`), where the
  name is the title. *Name it* is the same words, one place further.
- **The family** is reachable in the List (the folded rows show), not on the
  shelf: a spine's `+2` is a sticker. F6 moves it to Open as the spec says.
- **The dock's SIT DOWN link** becomes **Open** (words); the bar's own *Open
  the take ›* stays hidden under the rooms as it was (F5b makes the name the
  link).
- **The phone's deck card goes**, so the bar (the mini player) is no longer
  hidden on the shelves: it is the only transport there. Its strip is F5b's.
- **A tape in the bar** (the Pi's loaded tape, when no take is in it): the deck
  and the dock open the **Studio** (`/tape.html`, saying *for the tape* first,
  as GRAB does), not `wave.html?tape=` (F6's to remove).
- **The sketchbook** stays as it is (F6 decides the chair's fate); its tape
  route follows the deck's.
- **Tapes:** no shelf, no tape card, no *Swap it?* in the room. The Studio's
  title menu (`#tape-name`) lists every tape and loads one; it is not
  flag-gated and stays. `swap.js` keeps `loadTape` and `wireOpenInStudio` (the
  chair's *Load it in the Studio*, for `?tape=`, uses them until F6 removes
  that page) and loses `putTapeOn` and `recording`, which only the room's tape
  card used. The chair's own *Swap it?* question stays until F6.
- **Bounces** are takes folded into their tape's spine (F2); unchanged.

## Review fixes (PR #126)

- [x] **A take that can't play is not a dead end:** its toast has **Open**
  (`/wave.html?file=`); a take still encoding (or with no waveform yet) is also
  looked for every 1.5 s, ten times, and when it turns up it is put in the bar
