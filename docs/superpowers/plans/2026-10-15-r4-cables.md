# Cables as ropes (R4) — implementation plan

**Goal:** The rig's cables hang and swing as ropes, plugs snap into sockets
and come out with a pull, and nothing about what a cable is changes: each
is still a setting, and the keyboard still patches through P6's buttons.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-r4-cables-design.md` (the brief
is *R4 · Cables* in `2026-10-15-scene-contract-design.md`; the rig is
`2026-10-15-rig-design.md`)

## Global Constraints

- Behind `hindsight.rooms`: the rig is reached only with it on, and with it
  off `rig.html` says where the switch is and does nothing else, as in P6.
  No other page changes.
- The scene contract's rules: the page writes state, the `cable` model
  computes the rope, the renderer only draws. Reduced motion is the
  ticker's `dt = Infinity`; the ticker parks once every rope settles.
- Kinds keep P6's width and dash (`kinds.js`), and pending keeps its
  hollow, dashed look with open square plugs.
- The ropes never take input: P6's socket buttons stay the controls, for
  the keyboard and screen readers.
- CSS in one block at the end of `styles.css` (*Rooms R4*), every selector
  on a `rig-` class.
- `sw.js` → `hindsight-shell-v89`; every new file in `SHELL`. No new
  `lib/` directory, so the globs don't change.

---

- [ ] **Spec and plan.**
- [ ] **`lib/scene/cable.js` (tests first):** `CableModel` — 16 points,
  verlet at a fixed 120 Hz with the view interpolated, gravity, damping,
  four alternating constraint passes, ends pinned to `a` and `b`; length
  from P6's sag (`sag` moves here, `rig/cables.js` re-exports it); a
  `declared` cable is taut; a new cable starts at rest, or from `seed`;
  `dt = Infinity` lands on the rest shape; settles and returns `false`.
  Registered in `models.js`. Tests: frame-rate independence (20/30/60/120
  Hz and ragged, 0.01 px), settling, twelve ropes park the ticker,
  `dt = Infinity` against the settled shape (1 px), declared is straight,
  a seed is where it starts.
- [ ] **`lib/scene/plug.js` (tests first):** `SNAP_PX = 24`, `snap()`
  (nearest within 24 px of a hole, or over its button); `PULL_PX = 48`,
  `pull()` (in until 48 px, then out until back within 24 px), `lean()`
  (a seated plug leans toward the finger, a few px at most).
- [ ] **`lib/rig/ropes.js` (tests first):** `RopeRenderer` on the contract —
  `mount`, `frame` (only ropes whose view or state changed), `hit`, `twin`,
  `destroy`; kinds' width, dash and ink; hollow pending with open square
  plugs; nothing it draws takes pointer events. Tests with a stub DOM.
- [ ] **`lib/rig/page.js`:** a scene on the shared ticker; each graph cable
  a `cable` object, its ends measured from the holes and set only when
  they moved; a drag holds a new cable or the socket's one plug, snapping
  and pulling by `plug.js`; a drop's cable seeded from the rope in the
  hand; a tap on a rope through `hit()`; a ResizeObserver and
  `document.fonts.ready` re-measure. The legend uses the renderer's
  strokes. `.rig-hits` goes.
- [ ] **CSS:** *Rooms R4* block at the end of `styles.css`.
- [ ] **`sw.js`:** v89; `lib/scene/cable.js`, `lib/scene/plug.js`,
  `lib/rig/ropes.js` in `SHELL`.
- [ ] **Docs:** `development.md` (*The rig*, *The scene*).
- [ ] **Checks:** the node globs, `gofmt`, `go vet`, `go test -race ./...`.
- [ ] **In the browser:** twelve cables swinging at 4× CPU throttle, frame
  times reported; the ticker parks after; keyboard patching; screenshots at
  390×844 and 1440×900, both schemes, flag on and off, and one mid-drag.
