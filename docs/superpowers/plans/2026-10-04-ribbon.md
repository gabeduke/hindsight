# The ribbon — step 5 of the editing-model roadmap

**Spec:** [editing model](../specs/2026-10-03-editing-model-design.md), "New
capabilities" items 1 and 2; `Ring.Range` from the
[tape design](../specs/2026-10-03-tape-design.md). **Checks:**
[guide](../../guide.md) §6. **Branch:** `ribbon`, stacked on `undo-trash`.

## Decisions taken while planning

- **Spans are absolute ring frames**, the clock live flags already use. The
  envelope now says where "now" is (`total_frames`, `sample_rate`), so the
  page turns a point on the ribbon into a frame; a selection kept in frames
  slides left with the audio it names.
- **One route, two forms:** `POST /api/trigger?from=&to=` beside
  `?seconds=`. Saving from the ring is what trigger already means.
- **`Ring.Range(from, to, pick, emit)`** copies a chunk (32k frames) at a
  time under the lock, only the picked channels, re-checking the window each
  time; the saver writes each chunk straight to the `.part` file
  (`wavWriter`). A span the ring overwrites mid-save fails cleanly.
- **A start that aged out moves to the oldest audio** (plus 1 s once the
  ring is overwriting, re-checked just before the copy), and the answer says
  so; a span wholly gone is a 409.
- **Dated by its last frame's time** (clock bridge, else counted back at the
  sample rate), so a past span sorts by when it was played; the tempo is read
  over its own times. `MIDI_SNAP_BARS` doesn't move it. **Named by now**, like
  every take: a past name could be one a deleted take had.
- **Gestures:** hold (350 ms) then drag selects; hold without a drag selects
  from there to now; a tap or a vertical swipe does nothing (`touch-action:
  pan-y`). A bar under the ribbon shows the span and offers *Save as take*
  and ×.
- **A flag tap opens a sheet** (*Save from here to now*, *Delete flag*)
  instead of deleting at once.
