# Punch-in FX and tape mods (P4) — implementation plan

**Goal:** ROLL, FILTER, ECHO and CRUSH held like tricks, a varispeed bend,
and wow, flutter, drive and age saved in the tape, all rendered on the Pi in
the render-ahead path, with the click left dry.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-punch-in-fx-design.md`, with
the socket table in `2026-10-15-instrument-strip-design.md` (*The socket,
extended*).

Built in two halves. The engine half (Go, no UI) comes first, on its own
branch, and is rebased onto P3's strip to become `rooms/p4-fx`, where the
UI half is added.

## Global Constraints

- **A tape with no mods and no FX renders exactly as today, sample for
  sample.** Bypass is one branch per stage.
- The click stays dry: read head → mix → tape character → punch-in FX →
  click → `ToInt32`.
- FX and the bend are playback state: no undo, nothing written to
  `tape.json`, refused in a mixdown. Mods are the tape's: one undo per
  gesture, printed in a mixdown.
- Every engage, release and amount move is smoothed (a one-pole of about
  10 ms for amounts, 5 ms for engage and release), within the trick tests'
  `maxJump` bound.
- State crosses `advance`'s splits; a late render steps each stage's clock
  and drops an echo's tail.
- The play socket's changes are additive: the 1024-byte read limit, the
  16-slot `refused` channel and `playHolds` stay as they are.
- The engine half stays out of `web/static/`, so it rebases onto P3 cleanly.

## The engine half (`rooms/p4-engine`)

- [x] **Plan:** this file.
- [x] **Tape mods in the model:** `Style {formula, wow, flutter, drive,
  age}` in `State` (omitted when unset), the formulas table, `validate`,
  `clone`; `Mix.style`; `Engine.SetStyle`. Tests in `mods_test.go`.
- [x] **The new pads:** `roll`, `filter`, `echo`, `crush`, `bend` in
  `pads`; an amount per pad (`PressAmount`, `Set`), read at the press and
  moved while held; `mod.wow` … `mod.age` previews through the same inbox,
  coalesced so a flood of `set`s stays bounded; `at` events carry `v`, and
  amount moves are logged at most 30 times a second per pad.
- [x] **The read head:** ROLL as a head trick (the slice from the grid
  line where it lands, its length from the amount, changed at the next
  repeat by `set`); the bend as an offset on whatever is heard, ±8 %,
  rejoining the transport through a 5 ms crossfade; WOW and FLUTTER as a
  zero-mean offset through the Hermite path whenever they're up, the
  transport included.
- [x] **The FX bus** (`fx.go`): DRIVE and AGE, then FILTER (TPT state
  variable, 20 kHz to 200 Hz, a 1/16 reopen on release), CRUSH (12 to 4
  bits, the rate to an eighth) and ECHO (3/16 of a bar, 0.55 feedback,
  rings out to −60 dB), four channels each, bypassed when idle. DRIVE is
  made up to a fixed reference, a sine at −18 dBFS RMS (the demo showed the
  bare curve 27 dB louder; a follower ducked attacks). **For Gabe's ear:** at
  full drive a loud loop comes out about 10 dB quieter (at −6 dBFS RMS;
  quieter material is lifted up to 4 dB). The reference can move.
- [x] **`advance`:** tape → mods → FX → click in every branch; a mixdown's
  pass prints the mods and skips the FX; a late render steps the clocks.
- [x] **Checks** (`fx_test.go`, `mods_test.go`): back to identical after
  release and tail; bit-identical with every mod at 0; no clicks across
  engage, release and amount steps; the shape of each; FX never write
  `tape.json`; mods write once per gesture and undo restores them; a
  mixdown refuses FX and prints mods; splits and late renders; the random
  jam and the two-engine twin with the new pads.
- [x] **`BenchmarkRenderABlock`:** each FX, all four, mods on, everything at
  once.
- [x] **The socket:** `press {pad, q, v}`, `set {pad, v}`, `at {…, v}`;
  `release` of a `mod.*` lets its preview go, as a dropped connection does.
  Tests in `play_test.go`.
- [x] **The PATCH:** `PATCH /api/tapes?id=` `{style: {…}}` through
  `Engine.SetStyle`. Tests in `tape_test.go`.
- [x] **A controller's knob:** `ModKnobs` previews a mod as it turns and
  commits it after a second still, for P3's controller on the Pi.
- [x] **Docs:** `api.md` (the socket, the PATCH).
- [x] **End to end** against the demo: output in `both`, record
  `/api/tapes/stream` while a script holds pads with `v`, level per 50 ms.
- [x] `gofmt -l .`, `go vet ./...`, `go test -race ./...`.

## The UI half (`rooms/p4-fx`, on P3's strip)

- [x] **Pads:** ROLL, FILTER, ECHO, CRUSH in P3's `slot('fx')`
  (`lib/play/fx.js`, drawn with `screen.js`'s `addPad`), keys T Y U I
  (`controls.js`, no longer `soon`), each showing its amount and a meter. A
  press carries `v`: a velocity, or where the pad was left (0.5 to begin).
- [x] **Amounts:** `lib/play/amounts.js` keeps each one and sends a move no
  more than 30 a second, the last always; a finger slides a held pad's
  amount; the bend is a strip that springs back (`press`/`set`/`release`,
  landing at once).
- [x] **The mods panel** in `slot('mods')` (`lib/play/mods.js`): the
  formulas and four knobs, a drag previewed over the socket and one PATCH
  on the lift, the keys' steps saved once still; *custom* after a knob
  moves. On a phone or a tablet the mods are the panel's second page
  (*swipe for tape mods*, from the PlayPhone board).
- [x] **MIDI, in a browser** (`midi.js`): velocity, the mod wheel and
  aftertouch on held FX, the pitch wheel, CC 21–24 (saved after a second
  still) and program change 0–3.
- [x] **MIDI, in the Pi** (`internal/api/controls.go`): the same, with
  `tape.ModKnobs` committing a knob after a second still, as `midi:<port>`.
- [x] **The mirror:** pads lit only from an `at` without `move`; `move`s
  draw the amount on every screen; the strip puts its pads right against
  `live.tricks` on each poll (`lib/play/held.js`).
- [x] **A mixdown:** the FX dim and say they wait; a refused press says why
  in plain words.
- [x] Guide §8.13 and §9, `TIPS`, `sw.js` `CACHE` v81 with the new modules in
  `SHELL`, screenshots at both sizes and schemes, flag on and off.
