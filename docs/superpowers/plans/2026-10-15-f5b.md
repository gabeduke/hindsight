# F5b · The bar's strip, name, ✎ and Repeat; the sound at the tap and its flight — implementation plan

**Goal:** With `hindsight.rooms` on, a take in the bar shows ▶, the take as a
strip to tap or drag (on a phone's mini player too), its name (a tap opens it)
and ✎ (which takes the frame you are hearing, keeps it for the next page and
opens the take). The bar's ⟲ is **Repeat** for a take. A tap on a spine in the
room plays at once and a cassette flies from its shelf to the deck alongside the
sound. With the flag off: nothing changes.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-flow-design.md` — *A tap plays*
(*The bar is the only transport…*), *Open: one page to dig in* (✎), the words

decision 13 (*R2 starts the sound at the tap, not at SEAT; the flight runs
alongside it. R7 is dropped*), *What changes, page by page* (the bar row),
stories 5 and 6, *Build order* F5.

**Split.** F5 is two PRs; F5a (#126, `rooms/flow-f5a`) is below this one. F5b is
the bar side and R2's flight. F6 (not here) makes the take page take the

**Branch:** `rooms/flow-f5b` off `rooms/flow-f5a`. PR base `rooms/all`

## Global constraints

- Behind `hindsight.rooms`. Flag off = today's bar: no new element in its
  markup (the name link and ✎ are made in script, as F5a's Tag key is), no new
  listener, nothing written to storage. New CSS is scoped to `.np.np-take`
  (a class only the rooms' script adds) or to `room-` classes
  (AGENTS.md: a flag hides markup, not CSS).
- Tests are behavioural, on stand-ins for the document, the clock, storage and
  the bar (`lib/play/fake-dom.js`). Every page-wiring line has a test (or a
  headless-browser run) that goes red when the line is deleted; every new test
  is seen failing first.
- Every tip, guide sentence and label is true with the flag on and off.
- Never hide the only route to something; never override what the person did;
  touch targets ≥ 44 px on a phone; keyboard path always.
- New `lib/` files go in `SHELL`. No new `lib/` directory is expected
  (`lib/bar/`, `lib/room/`, `lib/scene/` are already in the node globs).

## Decisions the spec left open

- **Which bars.** The bar F5b changes is `NowPlaying` (`lib/bar/bar.js`), which
  Takes and Capture / the Studio use. The take page's and the tape page's bars
  are their own markup and are not touched: the take page *is* the take, so a
  name that opens it, and ✎ before F6's note, have nothing to point at; the
  Studio's tape keeps its words (*loop*).
- **The name and ✎ are made in script** (`lib/bar/take-keys.js`), after ⏏ in
  `.np-own`, only for a take, only with the rooms on. `#np.np-take` is the one
  class every rule hangs on.
- **The name is a link** (`wave.html?file=`): middle-click and the keyboard work;
  `Open the take ›` (`#np-open`) and the room's `Open` key stand down for a take
  (the name is the route and is always there); for the tape they stay (the
  Studio).
- **The phone's mini player for a take:** ▶, the name, ✎ in a row and the strip
  across under it. The reel window and its expand (▾) are not shown for a take:
  *there is no Open the player for a take*. The tape keeps the window, ▾ and
  the player. `--np-h` is taller while a take is in the bar.
- **✎** takes the take and the frame NOW, before anything else runs, keeps
  them (`stashFrame`, its own two-minute life, used once) and goes to the take
  page. Until F6 the take page does not read it, so ✎ simply opens the take
  at the bar's own place (F4). The accessible name is `Note at 1:23`.
- **⟲ is Repeat for a take, Loop for the tape** (the words table: the tape's
  loop is another thing). The label follows what the bar holds.
- **R2's flight is ported, not merged.** `choreo.js` (the Choreography's
  numbers) and `flight.js` (its model) come over from #116 with their tests;
  the deck's door, the card, the chair's eject and `Carry`'s SEAT timers do not:
  there is no door, no card and no SEAT to wait for. The sound starts at the
  tap; the flight starts after `toggle()` has been called and runs on the scene's
  shared ticker. It flies a ghost of the spine to the deck's art (a laptop) or to
  the bar (a phone, which has no deck) and dissolves into it, where the deck
  settles once. Where there is no spine on the page (a row of the List) or no
  rect to fly to, nothing flies.
- **No gap on the shelf.** The spine stays where it is, ringed (F5a decision 4):
  its ring is the "in the bar" mark and its name is on the ghost.
- **One flight at a time.** A tap on another take mid-flight lands the first at
  once (its spine's ring is already on) and starts the new one from its spine; a
  tap on the same take (a pause), a navigation (`pagehide`), a blocked `play()`
  or a deleted spine cancels it. Reduced motion: no flight and no ring pulse,
  just the deck updating. Idle: no scene, no ticker, no listener.
