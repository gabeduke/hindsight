# The instrument strip and the control bus (P3) — implementation plan

**Goal:** Play mode moves out of its overlay into a drawer on the Studio's
tape page: folded, one live row of pads; unfolded, the reel as the loop ring
and the whole instrument. One list of actions feeds touch, the computer
keys, Web MIDI and a controller plugged into the Pi, and MIDI LEARN binds a
controller's notes to it.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-instrument-strip-design.md`
(supersedes decisions 2 and 3 of `2026-10-14-play-mode-design.md`; fills in
*MIDI* from `2026-10-14-play-mode-sound-design.md`).

## Global Constraints

- Behind `hindsight.play` (`lib/play/flag.js`), this device only. With it
  off: no markup, no listeners, no socket, no requests; the tape page's keys
  are today's. `mount.js` builds into `#np-dock`; `#play-mode` and
  `enterButton()` go.
- The Studio's verbs (C catch, G grab, K keep, ↵ drop) are the rooms':
  their keys and pads need `hindsight.rooms` as well. K → KEEP and the click
  → M happen only with the rooms on, so a kit with both flags off keeps K as
  the click.
- `lib/play/controls.js` is the only list: `KEYMAP`, `COMMANDS` and the
  buffer's `BUFFER_KEYS` derive from it, and every action has a key or a
  MIDI default. The Pi's copy of the defaults (`internal/api/controls.go`) is
  held to it by a test that reads `controls.js`.
- The strip has the keyboard only while unfolded, and the KEYS lamp says
  when. Physical codes, repeats ignored, `typingInto`, and a lost window or
  hidden tab releases everything, as before. A key let up after the keys
  moved still releases its pad.
- Held stays held-only: nothing played touches undo or the tape.
- `play.go`'s changes are additive (learn, the controller holder, `held` in
  `hello`), so P4's engine half rebases cleanly. P4's pads (ROLL FILTER ECHO
  CRUSH on T Y U I), the bend and the mods are in the list, marked `soon`,
  and unbound until P4; the strip keeps an empty slot for them.
- CSS in one block at the end of `styles.css`, *Rooms P3*, every selector on
  a `play-` class, drawn in the `--pad-` materials (same in both schemes).
- `sw.js` → `v80`; every new `lib/play/` file in `SHELL`.

## Review: the strip and the buffer take turns

- [x] **The rule** (`turns` in `strip.js`, tested in `mount.test.js`): the
  strip and the buffer are never both in full. Unfolding the strip folds the
  buffer to its peek (a line on a laptop, the sheet's peek detent on a
  phone); bringing the buffer forward folds the strip; a verb the strip
  plays itself (GRAB) is the strip moving, so it stays.
- [x] **`buffer.js`** (additive): `hindsight:bench-forward` from a grab of
  this page's own and from its Buffer ▴ key. **`strip.js`** answers it, and
  a tap on the peeked buffer.
- [x] **Phone:** with the toggle in the mini player the sheet stands above
  it, giving up the player's height so its top edge stays; PLAY drops it to
  its peek and the open strip clears it. Tests in `lib/play/styles.test.js`.
- [x] **Laptop:** the peek is one line (name, length, AUDITION, ✕, *Open ▴*)
  and the lanes unfold; with the buffer the one open under 1000 px tall the
  feed folds to its keys and the bench closes up, so DROP, KEEP, TOSS and
  two lanes stay in view at 1440 × 790.
- [x] **Look:** 1440 × 790 and 900, 390 × 844, both schemes; the flags off
  lay the page out exactly as before (rects compared against the branch's
  previous head).
