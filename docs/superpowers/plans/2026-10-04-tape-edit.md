# Editing a tape — step 7a of the editing-model roadmap

**Spec:** [tape](../specs/2026-10-03-tape-design.md), *Phase 2: editing*;
[editing model](../specs/2026-10-03-editing-model-design.md), "The
clipboard". **Checks:** [guide](../../guide.md) §8.5. **Branch:**
`tape-edit`, stacked on `tape-clipboard`. Step 7b (mixdown and export)
follows.

## Decisions taken while building

- **The selection is the loop.** The OP-1 has In and Out; here the ruler's
  hold-and-drag already sets them, so lift and copy take the loop's span
  rather than a second selection.
- **One track or all, kept apart.** *All* lifts four tracks into a
  four-track clipboard, so a drop from track 1 puts each back on its own
  track (and drops silence over the span on tracks that had nothing).
- **Merge drop is a drop,** `{merge: true}` on `/api/tapes/drop`, not an
  edit: it shares the drop's playhead, first-loop and room rules.
- **Split cuts every layer** on the selected track at the playhead -- the
  OP-1's split is of the tape, not of one clip.
- **Join only rejoins a split:** the next clip on the layer must carry
  straight on in the same file at the same level and nudge, so a join never
  changes what's heard.
- **Slide snaps on the page** (bar, beat, eighth or off, remembered per
  device); the server takes the frame it's given and places the clip on the
  lowest layer free there. Each slide is its own undo step.
- **×2 replaces what follows the loop** on every track, like a drop, and is
  refused if the doubled loop wouldn't fit on the tape.
- **Lift writes the tape first, then the clipboard,** and undoes the lift if
  the clipboard can't be written, so audio is never only in neither place.

## After the independent review

Fixed: a tap on a clip opened its sheet on `pointerup`, so the tap's own
click then pressed a button in the sheet (taps are clicks again; only a held
drag is pointer-driven); a held tap that wobbled a pixel slid the clip (a
slide now needs a drag of 8 px, and an off-grid clip moves by whole steps,
keeping its offset); a second finger left a slide stuck (one pointer at a
time, by id, and a lost capture ends it); a lift whose clipboard couldn't be
written was rolled back by an undo that could pop someone else's edit and
left a redo (the clipboard is now written inside the edit); a slide to an
`at` near the int64 limit overflowed `End()` past validation; split ignored
nudges (it now cuts where a clip is heard). Also: Join works from either
half; Drop is disabled when a several-track clipboard doesn't fit from the
selected track; a clipboard fetch in flight can't undo an edit's newer one;
the docs no longer call a merge a bounce, since the tracks' mix doesn't come
with it.
