# F4 · The bar's hand-off between pages — implementation plan

**Goal:** With `hindsight.rooms` on, the bar carries its take across pages: a
new page's bar picks up at the same frame, plus the time away if it was
playing, and lands paused and ready (no error) where the browser won't start
sound by itself. The tape in the Studio is never displaced. A function takes
the bar's frame *now*, for ✎ in F5 / F6.

## The record

`sessionStorage['hindsight.bar.carry']` (per tab by nature; other tabs never
see it):

```json
{ "v": 1, "take": "jam_….wav", "frame": 123456, "sr": 48000, "playing": true, "at": 1760000000000 }
```

or `{ "v": 1, "for": "tape", "at": … }` (below). Frames are the take's own
(`takeSource.pos()`), `at` is `Date.now()` at the write.

- **When it is written:** `pagehide` **and** `visibilitychange` → hidden. On an
  iPhone, `pagehide` is not reliable when the page is swiped away or the app
  is switched; `visibilitychange` is the one that always fires. Both write the
  same record, the later overwriting the earlier, so a page that goes hidden
  and is navigated away from afterwards ends with the pagehide one. A page
  restored from the back-forward cache (`pageshow`, persisted) removes it: it
  is alive again and nobody should resume it from a copy.
- **A bar holding no take** (the tape, or empty) *removes* the record instead of
  leaving an older one, so a record never outlives the focus it describes.
- **When it is read:** once, in `pageBar`'s construction, before the bar draws
  anything. Reading removes it: **a reload cannot replay the same time away
  twice** (the reload's own `pagehide` writes a fresh record, which is the
  correct "carry" of a reload).
- **Ignored** (and removed): wrong shape or version, older than
  `CARRY_LIMIT_MS` (**5 minutes**: the take is a person's focus, not a
  bookmark; a coffee break is not a focus), dated more than 5 s in the future
  (a clock that moved), a take the page's list does not hold (deleted, not
  shelved on this page), or a playing take that **ended during the gap** (it
  was not on Loop; the bar has nothing to resume).
- **Maths:** paused → the frame. Playing → `frame + away × sr`, where
  `away = now − at`. The take plays at 1×; the gap is wall-clock.

## Decisions the spec left open

- **How a tab and a GRAB / Open in the Studio are told apart.** They are the
  same URL (`/tape.html`) and the same navigation, so the destination cannot
  tell them. The *leaving* page can: every route that goes to the Studio *for
  the tape* now says so just before it navigates (`forTheTape()` in
  `lib/bar/carry.js`: `notes.js` `sendToStudio` — every GRAB, from the sheet,
  the chair and the notes — and the two *Open in the Studio* clicks in
  `doorway.js` / `chair.js`, and the take page's *Open the tape* toasts). It
  writes `{for: "tape"}` over the record, which the pagehide that follows
  respects for 10 s (then a failed navigation doesn't leave it stuck) and
  which no page applies. Anything else that leaves — a nav tab, ‹, a link —
  carries the take.
- **The Studio is transparent, like the Rig and the guide** (review fix 1).
  `tape.html`'s bar is the tape's (it does not use `pageBar`), so it never
  reads the hand-off, and it no longer drops or writes it either
  (`studioVisit()`): the Room, back, finds its take; the record's own five
  minutes cover the gap. `pageBar` also refuses on Capture opened as the
  Studio when a tape is loaded there. *By its tab, the take reaching a tape-on
  Studio's bar* needs that bar to hold a take: M (a take mode on the dock) to
  L (moving it onto the shared `NowPlaying`; the ids overlap and every dock
  handler needs a take branch). Not built; F5 must do it or drop the Studio's
  ▶-in-toast.
- **The take page (`wave.html`).** It has its own clock, so it can't be handed
  the take yet (F6: Open "opens where the bar is"). It *writes* a record on
  leaving, but only once the person has done something there (played, or moved
  the playhead): then Room → Open → back carries the frame left on the take
  page. Opened and left alone it is transparent: it leaves the Room's record
  as it was, so the take is still there, playing on by the clock, when they
  come back. It reads none.
- **The refused play.** `audio.play()` rejects (Safari on an iPhone:
  `NotAllowedError`; Chrome without a prior tap: the same; also `AbortError`
  and anything else): the take lands at the frame it was left at (not
  frame + away: "the same spot"), paused, ▶ lit, no toast. Played through
  `audio.play()` directly, **not** the row's `toggle`, whose catch shows
  *Playback blocked*. If the person has moved the playhead meanwhile, the
  landing doesn't pull it back.
- **While the take is looked for** (the list must load first) the tape is not
  put in the bar, so a carried take doesn't flash the tape first. That caps
  only how long the bar is blank (`HOLD_MS`, 4 s): the take is still put in the
  bar whenever the list does arrive, within the record's own limit, unless the
  person has picked something else meanwhile (review fix 3).
- **The frame for ✎** (`frameNow`, `stashFrame`, `takeStashedFrame`): a separate
  record, `hindsight.bar.frame`, with its own limit (2 minutes: it is a click
  away from the page it is for). It holds the frame at the tap, not advanced
  by the gap, and is consumed once. F4 adds no ✎ and no Open page.
